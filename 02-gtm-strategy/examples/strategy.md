# GTM strategy: Acme Route Co
Website: https://acme.example

## Core offer
**Acme Route Planner**: Route planning software that builds daily delivery routes for regional fleets.
Outcome: Dispatchers stop building routes by hand and drivers get clear daily plans.
Price point: Annual subscription per vehicle

## ICP
Regional delivery and distribution companies in the US running their own fleet of 20 or more vans or trucks.
- Industries: Transportation, Wholesale distribution, Food distribution
- Employees: 50,1000
- Countries: US
- Exclude: acme.example

## Personas
### Operations leader
Titles: VP Operations, Director of Operations, COO
- On-time delivery
- Cost per stop

### Dispatch / fleet manager
Titles: Fleet Manager, Dispatch Manager, Transportation Manager
- Hours spent planning
- Driver overtime

## Pains
- **Routes are planned by hand in spreadsheets every morning** (Dispatch / fleet manager): Hours of dispatcher time every day and late starts for drivers
- **No clear view of cost per stop** (Operations leader): Pricing and expansion decisions are made on guesses
- **Driver overtime from inefficient routes** (Operations leader): Payroll creeps up and drivers burn out

## Front-end offers (cold traffic)
### 1. Free route teardown
- What they get: We re-plan one day of their real routes and show the side-by-side
- Piece of the core offer: The route optimization engine
- CTA: Want me to run one day of your routes through it?

### 2. Cost-per-stop calculator
- What they get: A simple sheet that shows their cost per stop from numbers they already have
- Piece of the core offer: The reporting module
- CTA: Want the calculator?

### 3. Dispatcher morning checklist
- What they get: A one-page checklist for planning routes faster tomorrow morning
- Piece of the core offer: The planning workflow
- CTA: Should I send it over?
